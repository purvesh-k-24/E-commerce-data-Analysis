# 🛒 E-Commerce Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an **E-Commerce Sales and Profit Analysis** using the Superstore dataset.

The project uses **Python, Pandas, and Plotly** to clean, analyze, and visualize the data. The main goal is to understand sales performance, profit trends, product categories, customer segments, and the relationship between sales and profit.


---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Analyze overall E-Commerce sales performance.
2. Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
3. Compare sales across product categories.
4. Analyze performance of different sub-categories.
5. Understand customer segment performance.
6. Visualize the relationship between sales and profit.
7. Generate useful business insights from the dataset.

---

## 🧹 Data Analysis

The project uses Pandas to load and analyze the Superstore dataset.

Main analysis includes:

### 📅 Monthly Sales Analysis

Monthly sales are calculated to identify:

* Highest sales month
* Lowest sales month
* Overall sales trend

### 📦 Category Analysis

Sales are analyzed across major product categories to identify the best and lowest-performing categories.

### 🏷️ Sub-Category Analysis

Sub-categories are compared based on their sales performance.

### 💰 Monthly Profit Analysis

Monthly profit is analyzed to identify:

* Highest-profit month
* Lowest-profit month
* Overall profit trend

### 📊 Category & Sub-Category Profit Analysis

Profit performance is compared across categories and sub-categories.

### 👥 Customer Segment Analysis

Sales and profit are analyzed for different customer segments.

### 📈 Sales vs Profit Analysis

The project analyzes the relationship between sales and profit using visualizations.
